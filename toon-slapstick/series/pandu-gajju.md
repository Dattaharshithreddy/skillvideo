# Pandu & Gajju (పాండు - గజ్జు): series setup

Silent slapstick shorts, 30-60 seconds, vertical 9:16, set in an old house in a small Telugu town.
No dialogue. Telugu proverbs and signs on the walls carry the jokes that words would.

## The world

**Ammamma's house, Chinna Veedhi (Little Lane).** An old tiled-roof house with a sea-green kitchen,
a yellow hall, a courtyard with a tulasi plant, a mango tree behind, and a terrace where things are
dried in the sun. Outside the gate: a lane with a tea stall, a temple bell, an auto stand.

Ammamma lives alone with her animals, and treats them as her grandchildren. She cooks, they eat,
somebody always gets blamed.

## The household

| Who | What they are | Wants | Running gag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pandu** | Big, calm panda. Round gold glasses, orange scarf. | Peace, a good meal, a nap. | Cleans his glasses slowly before he loses his temper. Sits down like a real panda when things go wrong. |
| **Gajju** | Baby elephant in a yellow cap. | Food. Always food. | Eyebrow waggle before mischief. Trunk is a hand, straw, hose and periscope. Flaps his ears to fly, briefly. |
| **Ammamma** | Grandmother. Green saree, silver bun. | A quiet, clean house. | Appears in the last five seconds with a ladle. She always knows who did it. |
| **Chintu** | The boy next door, seven. | To play, right now. | His cricket ball, kite or top always ends up where Pandu is resting. |
| **Laddu** | Fat orange cat. | Milk, and to do nothing. | Smug. Lets the others take the blame, then loses anyway. |
| **Bujji** | Small white rabbit from the garden. | Carrots. Safety. | Ears shoot straight up when scared. Faster than everyone. |

**The heart of it:** Pandu and Gajju are brothers in everything but species. Gajju starts the
trouble, Pandu pays for it, and they share the last bite. Nobody is ever really hurt and nobody is
ever really a villain.

## What makes it ours

- Friends, not enemies. Every episode ends with them together.
- Indian home life: dosa, laddu, mango, monsoon, Diwali, Sankranti kites, power cuts, gully cricket.
- Gags come from what only these characters can do (trunk, ears, belly, glasses), not from weapons.
- Tabla, flute and veena score instead of a Western orchestra.
- A Telugu line somewhere in every set that comments on the story.

## Season 1: ten episodes

Each follows: calm setup, trouble starts, it escalates in three steps, one big hit, a topper, a warm ending.

1. **The Dosa Snatch** (kitchen). Pandu flips the perfect dosa. A trunk rises from behind the table and takes it. Chase through the house, Pandu meets the fridge, they share the last bite.
2. **Mango Season** (garden). One ripe mango, too high. Gajju tries his trunk, then his ears. Pandu shakes the tree: every mango falls on Pandu except that one. Bujji hops off with it; they end up sharing mango slices three ways.
3. **Who Drank the Milk?** (kitchen). Ammamma leaves a pot of milk to cool. Laddu sips from the top, Gajju drinks through his trunk from behind the counter. Pandu, guarding, sees the level drop and can't work out how. Ammamma returns; everyone points at Pandu. A milk moustache gives the real two away.
4. **Afternoon Nap** (hall). Pandu settles on the cot. Chintu and Gajju play cricket in the lane; the ball keeps coming through the window. Third time Pandu catches it without waking, bats it back with his belly, and it's a six. He's carried out as the hero, still asleep.
5. **One Umbrella** (lane, monsoon). Rain. One umbrella, two friends, and Gajju's ears. Every plan to stay dry soaks Pandu more. They give up and sail paper boats in the puddle.
6. **Kite Day** (terrace, Sankranti). Chintu's kite is stuck on the tank. Gajju flies up to free it and gets tangled; now Gajju is the kite and Pandu holds the string. A rival kite appears.
7. **The Papad Guard** (terrace). Ammamma lays papads to dry and puts Pandu on guard. Laddu, Bujji and Gajju each take one in a different way while Pandu's eyes get heavier. He wakes to one papad left, and eats it himself just as Ammamma arrives.
8. **Hiccups** (courtyard). Gajju has hiccups; each one honks his trunk and lifts him off the ground. Pandu tries to scare them out of him: a mask, a paper bag, a bucket of water. The cure works. Then Pandu hiccups.
9. **Diwali Night** (courtyard, night). Lamps everywhere. Gajju lights one flowerpot firework; it chases Pandu round the tulasi plant like a comet. They finish with sparklers, drawing shapes in the dark.
10. **Ten Laddus** (kitchen). Ammamma makes ten laddus for the festival and counts them. Each time she turns her back, one goes: cat, rabbit, trunk. She counts again, more and more suspicious. Finale: one laddu left, five guilty faces, and she hands it to the only one who didn't steal.
