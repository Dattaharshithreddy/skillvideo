# Indian TV kids' cartoon style: drawing study (v1.0, 2026-11-11)

Harshith: "see Chhota Bheem and Chutki ... can you draw a boy and girl like those? First train yourself on how to
draw like those characters and design accordingly and keep that knowledge."

**What this is:** the drawing *style* of a successful Indian children's series, written down as rules we can apply to
our own characters. **What this is not:** a licence to copy. Style (proportions, line, colour approach) is fair to
learn; a specific character's face, hairstyle, costume, markings or name is not. Rule 18/32 in lessons learned still
applies: no Bheem, no Chutki, no dhoti-and-vest, no twin braids with a bindi, no recognisable sidekick.

## The rules (what the frames show)

### 1. Proportion
- **The head is big: about one third of the standing height** (about 3 heads tall). Kids in this style are not
  3.9 heads; big heads are the appeal.
- **The head is an upside-down egg: widest at the cranium (forehead/top), narrowing to a small rounded chin.**
  A head widest at the cheeks or jaw reads as a melon. This was the cause of "watermelon head".
- Body: narrow shoulders, slim chest, a small round belly that pushes forward a little. Short legs, small feet.
- Neck: thin and short, but visible.

### 2. Face
- **Eyes are the biggest feature:** two tall white ovals, close together (almost touching at the inner corner), set
  at or a little below the middle of the head.
- **Pupils are small** dark dots (about a third of the eye width), not big irises. Direction of look is very clear.
- Upper lid line thicker than the rest; no lower lid line.
- Brows: thin, high, short arcs well above the eyes; they move a lot for expression.
- Nose: a small curve or hook between and slightly below the eyes. Mouth small at rest; opens very wide for shouts.
- Ears: small, at eye level, simple C shapes.
- Girls: pink cheek circles, a few lashes as flicks. Boys: no blush.

### 3. Line and colour
- **Outline is thin and even** (much thinner than ours was), dark brown rather than black.
- Inner lines are fewer still: elbows, knees and fingers are shown by the outline, not by extra lines.
- Flat colours; shading is at most one slightly darker tone on the far side of a body part. No hard cel crescents.
- Clean saturated costume colours (orange, purple, yellow, green) against warm skin.

### 4. Pose and acting
- **Arms are always bent at the elbow** and held a little away from the body; never hanging straight down.
- Stance: feet apart, knees slightly bent, weight forward. Arms crossed, hands on hips, or one hand up gesturing.
- Heads tilt; the body leans into what they look at. Nothing is symmetric in a held pose.
- Hands are small and simple, mostly fists or open hands with clearly separate fingers.

### 5. What makes a character recognisable here (so ours must be different)
- One strong costume colour block, one hair silhouette, one accessory. Ours already differ: Kittu's orange
  T-shirt and blue shorts with a messy fringe; Minnu's purple dress, white collar and single side ponytail.
