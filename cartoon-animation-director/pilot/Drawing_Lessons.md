# Drawing lessons: how classic cartoons are built (v1.0, 2026-11-11)

Harshith: "get the drawing lessons first, check YouTube for drawing lessons or go through sources, and draw like how
they build Tom and Jerry ... and then try". Principles only. No studio character is drawn or copied.

## Sources looked at
- Preston Blair, *Cartoon Animation* (Blair animated at Disney and at MGM with Tex Avery). Contents list: construction of
  the head, line of action, the skeleton foundation, hand construction, the "cute" character. Hands are begun as a
  mitten, then the middle fingers are added, and the fingers are placed unevenly so they do not look monotonous.
- Step-by-step "how to draw Tom" guides (easydrawingtutorials.com, drawingtutorials101.com): every one starts with a
  loose circle for the head with a vertical and a horizontal guide line, simple circles/ovals for the body and the
  hands, lines for the arms, and only then the outline that joins the shapes.
- howtodrawforkids.com "How to draw cartoon hands" (9 steps, looked at): rounded-square palm, wrist, thumb from the
  low outer side, then the four fingers one at a time, each a sausage with a round tip, all slightly different lengths.
- howtodrawforkids.com "How to draw a cartoon kid" (looked at): head shape, body bean, face, hair, legs, arms, clothes.
- Proko, "How to Draw Cartoon Hands": cartoon hands are exaggerations of real ones; four fingers read better than five
  in cartoons; act the pose out with your own hand.
- Real cricket coaching (Sikana, studied frame by frame earlier): catch in front of the body, give with the ball.

## The working method (used for every new pose from now on)
1. **Line of action** first: one curve through the whole body.
2. **Big shapes**: head circle with cross lines, body bean, hips.
3. **Skeleton**: stick arms and legs with a dot at every joint. Check the pose reads as a stick figure. Arms must not
   cross unless the action needs it; elbows go where a real elbow can go.
4. **Hands as mittens** in the right place and the right size (cartoon hands are drawn big and simple).
5. **Thumb, then four fingers**, uneven lengths, round tips, little gaps between them.
6. Clean line over the construction. Then compare with the model sheet.
