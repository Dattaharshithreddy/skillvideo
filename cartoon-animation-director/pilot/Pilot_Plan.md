## 1. Why this story and not "The Milk Bowl"

The pilot specified earlier (`references/08-pilot-project.md`) needs a cat crouching and lapping in profile and Gaju squeezed behind a pot. Both exist only as review material. Following the decision of 2026-11-11, this pilot needs no new character asset. "The Milk Bowl" is deferred, not cancelled.

I also dropped my first idea, a ball landing on a dozing Bheemu, after reading the episode list again: episode 4 ("Afternoon Nap") already has a ball landing on sleeping Bheemu and a catch. This story has no hit on anyone.

## 2. The story

**One sentence.** Gaju wants Bheemu to play ball, does more and more to get his attention, and gets it only by accident when the ball comes down on Bheemu's book.

**In full.** A quiet afternoon in the courtyard. Bheemu is reading, completely absorbed. Gaju arrives with a ball and wants him to play. He bounces it neatly and looks across: Bheemu turns a page. He hops about with the ball held high and his ears flapping: without looking up, Bheemu raises the book between them. Gaju, put out, throws the ball down far too hard, and it goes up out of sight. Nothing comes down. Gaju gives up and sulks. Bheemu lowers his book and, for the first time, looks at his friend, and softens. At that moment the ball returns and lands on the open book, which snaps shut. A long look at the ball; a long look at Gaju; Gaju's ears go down. Then Bheemu puts the book on the floor, picks up the ball and bounces it, calmly and well. Gaju is astonished and delighted, and they play. Chintu comes in: "Bheemu! Give the ball!" It was his all along. They hand it back. Gaju points at the book. Bheemu looks at it. One more page.

**Chain.** Ignored → tries harder → throws too hard → ball leaves → (wait) → ball lands on the book → Bheemu's attention → he joins in. A delayed chain with a reversal: the one who would not play turns out to be good at it.

**Why it is ours.** It comes from who they are: Bheemu bookish, calm and slow to react; Gaju unable to leave anything alone, never mean. The comedy is in non-reactions and one slow burn. It uses the vertical frame (the ball leaves and returns through the top).

**What the animatic is.** Key poses that pop from one to the next in 2 to 6 frames, with the real shot lengths, cuts and pauses. A line of text is burned into every frame: "ANIMATIC - timing rough, not animation", with the shot name and film frame, so that notes can cite frames.

**Scratch sound.** A calm bed, ducked through the wait; a tabla tap for each step, hop and bounce; a rising slide as the ball leaves; one `pop` alone on the contact frame; three dry ticks in the slow burn; the existing voice file `c_ball` for Chintu; the standard closing cadence. It is there to mark timing. **None of it is approved, and none of it has been heard.**

**Questions the animatic is meant to answer.**

1. Is the wait (2.4 s) long enough to enjoy, or too long?
2. Does S3, a one-second cut to Bheemu not reacting, earn its place?
3. Is the reversal (4 s) worth its length, or should the passes be cut?
4. Does the ending land, or does it need Bheemu to actually pick the book up?
5. Is nine shots the right amount of cutting?

Where two timings are in doubt after viewing, both will be rendered and compared in playback, not decided from stills.

- In S8 and S9 Bheemu's arm with the ball looks long and stiff.
