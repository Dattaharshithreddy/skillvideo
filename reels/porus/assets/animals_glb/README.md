# Puru vs Alexander: animals for NOVA

Metres, Z-up in Blender (Y-up in glTF), feet on the ground at the origin, head facing -Y (Blender front), same convention as the soldiers.

- war_elephant.glb: realistic elephant, 3.1 m to the top of the raised trunk, red caparison with gold border.
- puru_elephant.glb: same elephant, maroon caparison and a gold howdah with canopy. Seat cushion top is at about z 3.2 m, centred on the back; Puru sits there.
- horse.glb: Bucephalus, black coat, red saddle cloth with gold border, 2.25 m to the ear tips.

Static poses, no skeleton.

## Source and licence
Base elephant (assets/nature/elephant.glb) and horse (assets/animals/Horse.glb) are from the Ethereal Engine "Open Asset Kit - Basic" (github.com/ir-engine/ir-engine-assets-basic). Its AUTHOR.MD states the assets come from Microsoft's retired Remix 3D library, released CC0. Caparisons, howdah, recolouring: dress_animals.py (this folder).
